## The SoftStack Shelf — Pocket Tools for Humans

Small, ethical tools with warmth. Can't scale won't scale. Don't play well with others. 

### Voice & Brain
- **[TalkType](https://talktype.app)** — Voice notepad with a little ghost. Live dictation, 96MB on-device Whisper mode, style transforms, and zero-account QR Passport sync. Also on **[macOS Menu Bar](https://talktype.app/mac)** & **[Chrome](https://talktype.app/extension)**.
- **[ProMapper](https://promapper.app)** — Workshop table that turns rambling conversations, meetings, or tabletop sessions into living node maps, action items, and chronicles.
- **[ZipList](https://ziplist.app)** — Walkie-talkie voice checklists with 4-word live co-op sync and zero login sludge.
- **[DaySay](https://daysay.app)** — Voice journaling that feels natural. Daily thoughts without the homework vibe.
- **[RiffRap](https://riffrap.app)** — Voice-to-lyrics scratchpad for musicians. Mumble gibberish, catch killer lines.
- **[Slide-o-matic](https://slideomatic.app)** — Voice-powered neo-brutalist slide deck engine.

### Privacy, Files & Print Magic
- **[QRBuddy](https://qrbuddy.app)** — Anti-hostage gradient QR studio. Links, WiFi, photo slideshows, audio mixtapes, and destructible file lockers.
- **[DrShrink](https://drshrink.app)** — Drop a file in, get a smaller one out. 100% client-side photo, audio, and doc compressor.
- **[cryptkeep](https://cryptkeep.app)** — Zero-knowledge AES-256 browser dead-drop vault (key stays in the `#` URL hash).
- **[metaflush](https://metaflush.app)** & **[metasplash](https://metasplash.app)** — Client-side metadata yin & yang: wipe tracking EXIF/GPS locally, or stamp copyright & ID3 tags onto art.
- **[Corruptor](https://corruptor.app)** — Drag it in, drag it out wrong. Cosmic-entropy glitch & databend studio.

### Mac Rituals & Toybox
- **[Spellbreak](https://spellbreak.app)** — Unskippable aurora screen breaks for macOS.
- **[NibNab](https://nibnab.app)** — Native Swift color-coded clipboard & screenshot collector with offline Vision OCR.
- **[Stargram](https://stargram.app)** — Sovereign ephemeris horoscope reader & shareable CRT cosmic art.
- **[Asciifier](https://asciifier.app)** — Figlet + lolcat ASCII art studio and retro arcade in the browser.
- **[iconmakeit](https://iconmakeit.app)** — Trace a drawing, dress up a mascot, ship a full PWA icon set.

### Open-Source Organs
- **[holopass](https://github.com/pibulus/holopass)** — Deterministic, pointer & gyroscope-tracked holofoil cards in Svelte.
- **[juicy-sounds](https://github.com/pibulus/juicy-sounds)** & **[weightless](https://github.com/pibulus/weightless)** — Mechanical keyboard & 0kb procedural Web Audio UI sound engines.
- **[corrupt-js](https://github.com/pibulus/corrupt-js)** & **[synesthetic-typing](https://github.com/pibulus/synesthetic-typing)** — Zero-dependency text glitching & keystroke particle trails.
- **[raya](https://github.com/pibulus/raya)** — Zero-dependency `<radio-widget>` Web Component with Shadow DOM isolation.

## Philosophy

Digital hygiene. Human scale. Fewer moving parts. More care in the parts that remain.

`compression = simplicity = elegance = grace = joy`
